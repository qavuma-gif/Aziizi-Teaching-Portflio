# Islamic History Timeline
A simple timeline for IGCSE Islamiyat students.

| Year (CE) | Event |
|---|---|
| c. 570 | Birth of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) in Makkah |
| 610 | First revelation in the Cave of Hira |
| 622 | The Hijra to Madinah (start of the Islamic calendar) |
| 630 | Peaceful conquest of Makkah |
| 632 | Death of the Prophet (PBUH) |
| 632-661 | The Rightly Guided Caliphs |
| 661-750 | Umayyad Caliphate |
| 750-1258 | Abbasid Caliphate |

## Revision questions
1. Why was the Hijra important?
2. Name the four Rightly Guided Caliphs.
