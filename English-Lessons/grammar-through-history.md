# Grammar Through History

Learn English grammar by reading short history facts.

## Lesson goals
- Recognise and use the past simple, past continuous and present perfect
- Use time words correctly (in, on, at, during, since, for)
- Build clear sentences about historical events

## 1. Past simple: finished events
- Uganda *gained* independence in 1962.
- The First World War *began* in 1914.

*Rule:* Verb + -ed (regular) or a special form (irregular: begin → began).

## 2. Past continuous: background action
- Leaders *were negotiating* when the crisis began.
- People *were living* under colonial rule for decades.

*Rule:* was/were + verb-ing.
