# IGCSE Modern History: Causes of the First World War

A short revision guide for IGCSE History students.

## Long-term causes (remember M.A.I.N.)
- *Militarism:* European powers built up large armies and navies.
- *Alliances:* Europe split into two blocs, the Triple Alliance and the Triple Entente.
- *Imperialism:* Rivalry over colonies created tension between the powers.
- *Nationalism:* Strong national pride, and in the Balkans the push for independence.

## Short-term trigger
- 28 June 1914: Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary was assassinated in Sarajevo.
- Austria-Hungary declared war on Serbia, and the alliances pulled other powers into the war.

## Exam tip
Don't just list causes. Explain how each one connects to the next and say which you think mattered most, with evidence.

## Revision questions
1. Explain why the alliance system made war more likely.
2. Was the assassination in Sarajevo the main cause of the war? Explain your answer.
