# Aziizi Teaching Portfolio

Hello! I'm Teacher Aziizi. Many students struggle to balance learning language skills while trying to master heavy academic subjects like History, Islamiyat and Social Studies. My approach solves this by combining them. I hold a Bachelor of Education (BEd), a TEFL certification, and 8 years of teaching experience.

This repository contains sample lessons and study guides I created for Cambridge IGCSE students.

## What's inside

- *English-Lessons/*: conversational storytelling, grammar through history
- *History-Social-Studies/*: IGCSE modern history revision guide
- *Islamiyat-Studies/*: Islamic history timeline
- Geography

## Subjects I teach

Cambridge IGCSE History, Social Studies, Islamiyat/IRE and Conversational English.
