# Conversational English: Storytelling Practice

A speaking lesson to help learners tell stories clearly and confidently.

## Lesson goals
- Tell a short story in order, with a beginning, middle and end
- Use past tense correctly
- Use linking words to sound natural

## Useful linking words
- *First, / At the beginning,*
- *Then, / After that,*
- *Suddenly, / Meanwhile,*
- *In the end, / Finally,*

## Activity 1: Model story
Read this example aloud:

"Last weekend, I went to the market. First, I bought some fruit. Then I met an old friend, and we talked for an hour. In the end, I was late for lunch, but I was happy."

## Activity 2: Your turn
Tell a 5-sentence story about one of these topics:
1. A day you will never forget
2. A trip you took
3. A time you helped someone
